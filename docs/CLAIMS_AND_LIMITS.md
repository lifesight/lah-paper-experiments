# Claims and limits

The inspected architecture separates execution records, scoped workflow state, human authorization, coded findings and model judgments. Inspection shows interfaces, not deployed reliability.

The current empirical unit is NumberLedger numeric support. At 800 sources, exact direct claims receive 382/1,000 acceptance, compact direct claims 558/1,000, sums 1,000/1,000, signed differences 35/1,000 (including one exemption), named ratios 378/1,000 and percentage displays 134/1,000. Semantic errors in entity, date, currency or sign can still pass when magnitudes remain explainable. These acceptance rates are not factual-accuracy rates.

The percentage generator covers revenue/spend multipliers 3.01–8.98 displayed as roughly 301–898%, above the component's absolute-percentage <=200 exemption. These results do not characterize ordinary 0–100% shares. Generation and labels share derivation/rounding helpers; independence is from the tested matching logic, not a separately implemented oracle.

The recorded reconstruction has 290 sign-loss exposures across all temporal-prefix checks, including future-source checks; 254 occur in the ingested subset. The 290 must not be described as a count restricted to 14,675 ingested exposures. Exemptions bypass evidence checking. Positive magnitude support does not establish signed or semantic correctness.

Marketing measurement remains the application: average modeled ROAS, marginal modeled ROAS, platform attribution and experimental incrementality are distinct objects. The study does not validate underlying models/causal estimators, business decisions, full-harness correctness, live-model performance or customer lift. Seven groups from one development workspace do not provide population coverage. Current-query identity changes, shared helpers, repeated cases and finite grammar limit causal/mechanistic/generalization conclusions. No ablation benefit or superiority over other assistants is claimed.
